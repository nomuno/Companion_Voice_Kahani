Companion - Voice: Kahani
=====================
Optional voice pack for the Companion addon (WoW: Forever). Kahani is natural and bright, the default voice.

Companion already includes every voice's everyday lines (greetings, banter, praise, combat,
loot, level-up, reminders, guide lines). This pack adds Kahani's 1052 zone comments and zone
histories, so zone lines are spoken instead of shown as text only.

INSTALL
1. Close WoW completely.
2. Unzip so you get World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/Companion_Voice_Kahani
   (next to the Companion folder).
3. Start WoW. New addon folders need a full restart, not /reload.
4. In Settings > AddOns > Companion > Audio, Kahani shows "Installed - full pack".
   Pick her there or type /comp voice kahani.

Requires Companion 0.72 or later. Uninstall by deleting the folder.
